Work & Whisk – UX/UI Case Study

Problem

Traditional study timers feel robotic and transactional. I wanted to redesign productivity as something emotional, aesthetic, and rewarding.

Concept

Users set a study duration and watch their matcha visually evolve as they stay focused — turning time into a calming ritual.

UX Strategy

  • Visual reward loop
  • Minimal distractions
  • Clear primary action (Start Timer)
  • Soft calming color palette

Design Thinking

The goal was to reduce stress associated with studying and instead associate focus with warmth and comfort.

Tech Used

  • React (if applicable)
  • Simple UI logic for timer state
  • Visual progress transitions

What I Learned

Emotional design can significantly impact user motivation. Productivity tools don’t need to feel sterile — they can feel human.
