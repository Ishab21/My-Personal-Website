Sip Theory

Sip Theory is a concept boba brand website I built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, focusing on immersive storytelling, responsive design, and refined UI interactions. This project was especially meaningful to me because it combined something I genuinely enjoy with creative and technical problem-solving, allowing me to turn a familiar, personal experience into an engaging digital product. Having worked part-time in boba shops for the majority of my teen years, cafés have always held a special place for me. These roles challenged me in fast-paced environments and helped me grow into a more responsible, adaptable, and quick-thinking individual.

Work and Whisk!

Work and Whisk is a cozy, interactive study timer that transforms focus into a visual ritual. Users set a study duration, press start, and watch their virtual matcha gradually become richer, foamier, and more whisked the longer they stay focused. By turning productivity into a calming, aesthetic experience, Work and Whisk encourages users to slow down, stay present, and build consistent study habits through gentle visual reward.
