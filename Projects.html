What makes Sip Theory “work”

These are the core UX choices that drive the feel of the product.

Story-first structure

Instead of a typical menu + sections, the page is paced like a narrative. Copy blocks are short, scannable, and intentionally sequenced so users can “discover” the brand, not just read about it.

Responsive hierarchy (not just responsive layout)

On smaller screens, key actions stay visible and text hierarchy tightens. That keeps the experience calm and readable instead of turning into an endless scroll wall.

Micro-interactions with restraint

Motion is used to guide attention (hover states, subtle transitions, section emphasis), not to decorate. This keeps it feeling “premium café” rather than “loud landing page.”
