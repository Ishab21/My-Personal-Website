Problem Statement

Many café concept sites look beautiful but lack clarity: users can’t quickly understand what the brand is, what makes it different, or what to do next—especially on mobile. Sip Theory needed to feel immersive while remaining scannable and intuitive.

The Solution

  • Story-first layout with clear checkpoints (what → why → next action).
  • Calm hierarchy and spacing for fast scanning.
  • Subtle micro-interactions for affordance (not decoration).
  • Responsive decisions that protect readability and tap targets.

STEP 1

Discovery + Competitive Scan

What I noticed: Visual-first sites often break on mobile with long copy and unclear next steps.
Insight: Users decide quickly from social links—clarity first, story second.
Direction: Editorial pacing + checkpoints + restrained motion.

STEP 2

Ideation

IA method: Each section answers one question (What is this? Why care? What next?).
Feature choices: short paragraphs, CTA visibility, consistent spacing rhythm.
Motion rule: use animation for affordance only.

STEP 3

UI + Build Decisions

Typography: a premium “café calm” hierarchy.
Responsive UX: protect line length, spacing, tap targets.
Implementation: semantic HTML + CSS layout/motion + JS behaviors.

Challenges & Learnings

Challenges

  • Balancing poetic storytelling with scannability.
  • Keeping motion premium instead of noisy.
  • Maintaining readability across breakpoints.

What I Learned

  • Clarity creates trust—then users stay for the story.
  • Restraint often reads as more premium.
  • Responsive design is UX, not just layout.
