Sip Theory – UX/UI Case Study

Problem

Many café websites focus heavily on aesthetics but lack clarity in navigation and storytelling structure. I wanted to design a brand experience that felt immersive while still being intuitive and usable.

Research & Inspiration

I studied modern café and lifestyle brand sites, identifying patterns in visual hierarchy, whitespace usage, and micro-interactions. I noticed many sites were visually strong but lacked responsive clarity.

Design Decisions

  • Strong hero section to establish mood immediately
  • Soft earthy color palette for warmth
  • Clear typography hierarchy for scannability
  • Subtle animations instead of overwhelming effects

Tech Stack

  • HTML for semantic structure
  • CSS for layout, responsiveness, motion
  • JavaScript for UI interactions

Challenges

Balancing storytelling with usability was the biggest challenge. Early drafts were too narrative-heavy. I refined spacing, shortened copy, and improved call-to-action visibility.

What I Learned

Good UX is not just visual — it’s about pacing, clarity, and emotional flow. Restraint in design often creates a more premium experience.
