UX CASE STUDY

Sip Theory is a story-led boba brand concept site designed to feel immersive but still easy to navigate. I focused on visual hierarchy, responsive layout decisions, and small interactions that guide attention calmly.

Visit site Jump to Ideation

Problem

Many café brand websites look visually strong but feel unclear on mobile—users can’t quickly understand the brand, find what they need, or know what action to take next.

Solution

  • Structured the site like a story with clear checkpoints.
  • Designed typography + spacing for quick scanning.
  • Added subtle interactions for guidance (not distraction).

Tech Used

  • HTML (semantic structure)
  • CSS (layout, responsive design, styling)
  • JavaScript (small UI interactions)

How users should feel

  • Warm + welcomed (like walking into a café)
  • Curious to explore
  • Calm because everything feels clear
STEP 1

Discovery

Research + observation

Thinking Process

I explored café/lifestyle brand sites and focused on what makes users stay or leave quickly. The biggest pattern I noticed: visuals were strong, but the hierarchy wasn’t mobile-friendly. That shaped my approach—clarity first, story second.

Typography + Style

  • Large headline for instant brand impression.
  • Short readable paragraphs (max line length).
  • Warm neutral palette to feel cozy + premium.

1. Structure the story (without losing clarity)

I mapped the flow like a guided experience: introduce the brand, reinforce the vibe, then lead the user to the next action. The goal was to keep it immersive but still scannable.

2. Cognitive mapping for tone of voice

I defined the “Sip Theory mood” as warm, editorial, and calm. That informed typography choices, spacing, and micro-interactions. Everything needed to feel premium, not loud.

  • What emotions should the user feel as they scroll?

3. Feature selection (why these features)

I intentionally kept features minimal: smooth scrolling, clear CTA placement, and gentle hover states. I avoided anything flashy that could break the calm experience.

STEP 3

Design + Build

Implementation + refinements

Challenges

  • Making storytelling feel clear (not paragraph-heavy).
  • Keeping the design premium on mobile breakpoints.
  • Choosing interactions that guide, not distract.

What I improved after testing myself

  • Shortened text blocks and added breathing room.
  • Adjusted font sizes for small screens.
  • Moved CTAs to appear earlier in the scroll.
